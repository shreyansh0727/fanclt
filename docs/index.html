How the wiring works

ESP32-C6-Pico → 4-channel opto relay board → Fan + Light 1/2/3, with the OLED and the MP3 module. Flip a channel: the dashes are current, the faded parts are dead.

Pick a channel above, or press Auto demo. Watch the low-voltage side here, then open the mains tab.

Active LOW

The ESP32 pulls the input low (sinks a few mA) to energise a relay. 3.3 V on the pin means off.

4 × 10 kΩ pull-ups

Each IN also has 10 kΩ to 3V3 (one is drawn in grey above). That holds the inputs high while the ESP32 resets, so no relay clicks on boot.

Jumper removed

VCC stays on 3V3 (opto side). JD-VCC comes from the 5 V supply, so the opto LED sees a true 0 V when the pin goes low. GND stays shared.

~72 mA per coil

Four coils ≈ 290 mA. The 1 A adapter also runs the DFPlayer, which is why the coils do not hang off the ESP32's 3V3.

Only L is switched

Live runs L → 10 A fuse → COM, and NO → load. Neutral is common to every load and never passes through a relay.

One fuse for the board

The 10 A fuse sits before the COM chain, so a short in any channel takes the whole board down instead of the house.

Inductive loads

A fan arcs across the contacts when it opens. Fit an RC snubber / MOV across COM–NO and derate the 10 A contact rating.

Separation

Mains lives in one compartment, ESP32 + 5 V in another. PE bonds the metal enclosure (Class I loads only). Never on a breadboard.

OLED: 4 wires

VCC on 3V3 (not 5 V), SDA GP22, SCL GP23. SH1106 at 0x3C, sharing the bus with the onboard 0x20 expander. 100 kHz is deliberate.

MP3-TF-16P

RX ← D18 through 1 kΩ (fine without it at 9600 baud and a short wire), TX → GP19, BUSY → GP21 (low while a clip plays).

SD card is mandatory

FAT32, 32 GB max, clips as /mp3/0001.mp3 … 0057.mp3. The firmware is useless for announcements without it.

Speaker

SPK1/SPK2 → 8 Ω 0.5 W. Power the module from the same 5 V rail as JD-VCC, never from 3V3.
